# LinearRAG reproduction research report

This directory is the externally readable research layer of the reproduction project. It will present the Q1-Q8 evidence chain in a form suitable for academic discussion and postgraduate interview review.

Its purpose is to explain:

- why each experiment was designed;
- where it belongs in the research process;
- which claim it tests;
- what the experiment observed;
- how the observation compares with the paper;
- which explanations are supported or still hypothetical;
- what the result implies and what it does not establish.

Future material may include question-level reports, curated tables and figures, representative cases, and presentation assets. Raw logs, predictions, caches, local paths, secrets, and unreviewed intermediate output do not belong here.

Every quantitative claim must be traceable to an internal record under `project/agent/experiments/` and from there to raw evidence under `data/output/`.
