# Q3 bounded formal run: 10% sample

## Status

Completed on 2026-09-20.

Raw evidence:
`data/output/experiment_results/q3_ablation_study/q3-formal-10pct-20260920/`

## Effective protocol

- 100 fixed-seed questions per dataset, 400 questions total.
- Datasets: HotpotQA, 2WikiMultiHopQA, MuSiQue, and Medical.
- Variants: full LinearRAG, without entity activation, and without global
  importance aggregation.
- One index load/build per dataset; all three variants reused the same model
  instance and graph for that dataset.
- Retrieval: official BFS path, top five passages.
- Embedding model: `all-mpnet-base-v2`.
- Generation and LLM evaluation model: `qwen3.8-flash`.
- Parallel workers: 16.
- Primary metric: mean of LLM and contain accuracy for HotpotQA, 2Wiki, and
  MuSiQue; LLM accuracy only for Medical.

## Results

| Dataset | Full | Without entity activation | Full minus ablation | Without global importance | Full minus ablation |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| HotpotQA | 80.0% | 73.5% | +6.5 pp | 74.0% | +6.0 pp |
| 2WikiMultiHopQA | 77.5% | 75.0% | +2.5 pp | 75.0% | +2.5 pp |
| MuSiQue | 54.5% | 54.0% | +0.5 pp | 56.5% | -2.0 pp |
| Medical (LLM accuracy) | 71.0% | 74.0% | -3.0 pp | 70.0% | +1.0 pp |

The two ablations reduced the primary score on HotpotQA and 2Wiki. The effect
was negligible or reversed on this MuSiQue and Medical sample. The local run
therefore does not reproduce the paper's claim that both modules improve every
dataset.

## Retrieval changes

| Dataset | No entity activation: mean Top-5 overlap / changed sets | No global importance: mean Top-5 overlap / changed sets |
|---|---:|---:|
| HotpotQA | 0.776 / 75 | 0.496 / 95 |
| 2WikiMultiHopQA | 0.822 / 63 | 0.444 / 96 |
| MuSiQue | 0.658 / 86 | 0.534 / 91 |
| Medical | 0.744 / 75 | 0.482 / 99 |

Both modules materially changed retrieved context even when the final accuracy
difference was small or reversed. Removing PPR produced the larger retrieval
change on every dataset.

## Paired outcome audit

The paired exact McNemar-style audit compared correct/incorrect outcomes on the
same questions. Only the HotpotQA contain-accuracy comparison for removal of
entity activation was distinguishable at the conventional 0.05 threshold
(`8` full-only correct, `0` ablation-only correct, exact `p=0.007812`). Other
comparisons did not reach that threshold. These are bounded-sample audit values,
not preregistered confirmatory tests.

## Interpretation boundary

This run establishes bounded local evidence under `qwen3.8-flash`, 100 sampled
questions per dataset, and the released BFS implementation. It does not
reproduce the paper's 1,000-question `gpt-4o-mini` protocol. The no-global
variant remains an operational interpretation of the paper description because
the authors did not publish their ablation implementation.
