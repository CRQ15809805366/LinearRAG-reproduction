# Q2 bounded formal efficiency run: 100 questions

## Status

Completed on 2026-09-20.

Raw evidence:
`data/output/experiment_results/q2_efficiency_analysis/q2-formal-100q-20260920/`

## Effective protocol

- Dataset: complete 2WikiMultiHopQA corpus of 658 passages.
- Questions: 100 fixed-seed questions.
- Retrieval: official BFS path, top 5.
- Parameters: `max_iterations=3`, `passage_ratio=0.05`,
  `iteration_threshold=0.4`, and `top_k_sentence=1`.
- One untimed warm-up followed by five timed retrieval repetitions.
- Reused the completed cache from the successful first-stage cold-index run;
  no second cold index was created.
- The measured 2.417 seconds is cache restoration and graph reconstruction,
  not cold indexing time.
- Three independent cold-index observations were completed in separate empty
  cache directories. The first came from `q2-design-check-20260920`; the two
  additional runs are `q2-cold-index-repeat-2-20260920` and
  `q2-cold-index-repeat-3-20260920`.
- `NoLLM` dynamically rejected any generative-model call.

## Interpretation

Executed evidence shows stable local retrieval latency across the first four
runs, with a slower fifth run. The median is close to the first-stage
10-question median of 0.152212 seconds/question, so increasing the sample to
100 questions did not materially change the observed per-question latency.

The paper reports 249.78 seconds for indexing and 0.093 seconds/question on
different hardware. The local medians are approximately 2.45 and 1.66 times
those values, respectively. These differences are descriptive, not evidence of
an implementation defect, because the hardware and detailed runtime
environment are not matched.

This run establishes the local LinearRAG result only. It does not execute the
GraphRAG baselines and therefore does not reproduce the paper's comparative
efficiency ranking.
