# Q1 bounded run: 10% sample

## Status

Completed on 2026-09-17.

Raw evidence: `data/output/experiment_results/q1_generation_accuracy/q1-formal-10pct-20260917-r2/`

## Effective protocol

- 100 fixed-seed questions per dataset, 400 questions total.
- Datasets: HotpotQA, 2WikiMultiHopQA, MuSiQue, and Medical.
- Methods: frozen top-5 Vanilla RAG and original LinearRAG.
- LinearRAG retrieval: official BFS path.
- Embedding model: `all-mpnet-base-v2`.
- Generation and LLM evaluation model: `qwen3.8-flash`.
- Temperature: 0.
- Qwen thinking mode: disabled with `enable_thinking=false`.
- Parallel workers: 16.
- Medical reports LLM accuracy only.

## Results

| Dataset | Vanilla Contain | LinearRAG Contain | Delta | Vanilla LLM | LinearRAG LLM | Delta |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| HotpotQA | 50% | 65% | +15 pp | 62% | 84% | +22 pp |
| 2WikiMultiHopQA | 65% | 75% | +10 pp | 62% | 76% | +14 pp |
| MuSiQue | 55% | 57% | +2 pp | 54% | 57% | +3 pp |
| Medical | not reported | not reported | not reported | 65% | 76% | +11 pp |

Executed evidence: LinearRAG exceeded Vanilla RAG on every reported metric in
this fixed 100-question sample. The margin was large on HotpotQA and 2Wiki,
small on MuSiQue, and 11 percentage points on Medical LLM accuracy.

This is a bounded Qwen-based comparison, not a reproduction of the paper's
1,000-question `gpt-4o-mini` table and not evidence against all GraphRAG
baselines.

## Paired outcome audit

Both methods answered the same 100 question IDs in every dataset.

| Dataset | Metric | LinearRAG-only correct | Vanilla-only correct | Same outcome | Exact paired p |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| HotpotQA | Contain | 18 | 3 | 79 | 0.001490 |
| HotpotQA | LLM | 28 | 6 | 66 | 0.000195 |
| 2WikiMultiHopQA | Contain | 15 | 5 | 80 | 0.041389 |
| 2WikiMultiHopQA | LLM | 22 | 8 | 70 | 0.016125 |
| MuSiQue | Contain | 10 | 8 | 82 | 0.814529 |
| MuSiQue | LLM | 13 | 10 | 77 | 0.677639 |
| Medical | LLM | 20 | 9 | 71 | 0.061428 |

The paired evidence is strong for HotpotQA and supports a positive difference
for 2Wiki. The MuSiQue difference is not distinguishable from sample variation
in this run. Medical is directionally positive but borderline at the conventional
0.05 threshold. These p-values are an audit aid, not a preregistered confirmatory
analysis and not a substitute for a second independent sample.

## Runtime interruption and recovery

The first formal attempt stopped during HotpotQA NER indexing with a NumPy
allocation error when spaCy attempted an approximately 340 MiB intermediate
array. NER batch size was reduced to at most eight documents; this changes
resource use, not the NER or retrieval algorithm.

The second attempt completed HotpotQA and 2Wiki, then was manually terminated
after an interrupted terminal wait left its process running. Resume support was
added at dataset and method boundaries. The resumed command skipped the two
completed datasets, continued from MuSiQue, and completed Medical. NER indexing
now exposes document-level progress. Failed run directories were deleted;
complete model stores and successful evidence were preserved.
